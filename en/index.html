American Dynamism2025

The Resurgence of Nuclear

AI's energy demands are driving a revival of nuclear power. Data centers need clean, consistent energy, spurring reactor orders for the first time in decades through regulatory reform, public support, and capital investment. The reopening of Three Mile Island exemplifies this trend toward securing America's AI leadership.

trending_upWhat actually happened

The prediction held up strongly. Constellation's 20-year power purchase agreement with Microsoft is reviving Three Mile Island Unit 1 (renamed the Crane Clean Energy Center), with all 835 MW dedicated to Microsoft's AI data centers and restart pulled forward to 2027. In November 2025 the Trump administration's DOE backed the project with a $1 billion federal loan, underscoring nuclear's central role in powering AI.

Bio + Health2025

Big is Back: Tackling Common Diseases (Again)

GLP-1 drugs are creating a $100B+ market by 2030, and breakthrough CAR-T therapies for autoimmune diseases are inspiring new biotech innovation. A German physician's results showed all 15 treatment-resistant patients improved dramatically, signaling a renewed focus on major disease areas.

trending_upWhat actually happened

GLP-1 drugs kept expanding through 2025, with the global GLP-1 receptor agonist market estimated at roughly $63-66 billion and projected to grow at a double-digit CAGR toward $185 billion by 2033. Novo Nordisk's Wegovy grew about 18% year-over-year in Q2 2025 and Ozempic remained near $4.7 billion per quarter, though growth cooled and forecasts were trimmed amid competition and compounded-drug pressure; an estimated 25 million Americans are projected to be on GLP-1 therapy by 2030.

Crypto2025

Enterprises Will Increasingly Accept Stablecoins for Payments

Stablecoins found product-market fit as the cheapest way to send a dollar and as an open platform for new payments products, but large enterprises have not yet awakened to the cost savings available by switching rails. In 2025, expect a bigger wave of experimentation, with small and medium businesses like restaurants and coffee shops switching from credit cards first, since they bear high fees but gain little fraud protection in person. Larger enterprises will also adopt stablecoins to disintermediate payment providers and capture margin, while seeking new solutions for fraud protection and identity.

trending_upWhat actually happened

Enterprise stablecoin acceptance accelerated sharply in 2025. Stripe closed its ~$1.1 billion acquisition of stablecoin infrastructure firm Bridge in February 2025 and rolled out worldwide USDC-settled payments letting merchants accept digital dollars on Ethereum, Base and Polygon at a flat 1.5% fee; within a week payments flowed from 70+ countries (later 120+). Stripe also launched a stablecoin-focused blockchain, Tempo, while surveys showed 6 in 10 Fortune 500 executives pursuing blockchain initiatives.

Growth2025

The Decline of "Google It"

Google's search dominance faces unprecedented pressure from chatbots and specialized answer engines. ChatGPT has 250+ million weekly users while Perplexity grows 25%+ monthly. Domain-specific providers like Consensus and Harvey serve niche needs, further fragmenting search.

trending_upWhat actually happened

The prediction was borne out: Google's global search share dipped below 90% for most of 2025, a level not seen since 2015, as AI-native tools gained ground. On a broader information-discovery basis combining search and AI sessions, Google's share fell from 89.3% in December 2022 to 57.6% by December 2025, while Perplexity grew roughly 370% year-over-year, confirming real erosion of Google's discovery monopoly.

Infrastructure2025

Hypercenters: The Regional Race for AI Infrastructure

AI Hypercenters are regions with 3-6 gigawatts of installed capacity to develop, train, and host frontier models. Nations including the U.S., China, Japan, Singapore, and Saudi Arabia are competing to reach this scale, viewing compute capacity as critical strategic infrastructure.

trending_upWhat actually happened

The gigawatt-scale buildout materialized faster than expected. OpenAI's $500 billion Stargate project with Oracle and SoftBank brought its flagship Abilene, Texas site online in September 2025 (scaling toward 1.2 GW), and the partners announced five new U.S. sites that push planned capacity to nearly 7-9 GW across Texas, New Mexico, Ohio and the Midwest, on track to hit the 10 GW, $500B commitment ahead of schedule.

Infrastructure2025

Small, But Mighty: On-Device AI

Smaller on-device models will dominate by usage volume, driven by privacy, economic, and practical considerations. Developments in frameworks like TensorFlow Lite and custom hardware such as the Google Edge TPU will enable responsive experiences on smartphones and IoT devices.

trending_upWhat actually happened

On-device AI advanced as predicted: Apple opened its ~3B-parameter on-device foundation model to developers via a new Foundation Models framework in 2025, and Google shipped Gemini Nano for fully offline summarization and rewriting through ML Kit's GenAI APIs. Small models like Microsoft's Phi-4, Gemma and Qwen now run quantized on phones, though local adoption in 2025 was still more bleeding-edge than turnkey due to hardware fragmentation and tooling immaturity.
